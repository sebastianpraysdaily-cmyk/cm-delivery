# "Get Ready With Me (For The Last Time)" (DRAFT v1, from the user's idea)

Format: a get-ready-with-me (GRWM) spoof. Kevin breaks the 4th wall and talks to us; the narrator eggs him on. 30 fps, 1080x1920, about 55 s.

## Platform-safety calls (edgy but not flagged)
- The cliff daydream is shown as a fantasy escape, not a crash or a death. The car launches off the cliff, then sprouts
  wings and glides to a beach. A car that drives off a cliff and crashes reads as self-harm, which gets videos
  age-restricted, demonetized or removed.
- The roasts hit behaviours, clothes and habits. Swearing is OK. No slurs, race or other protected traits, and no threats.
- No real brands or real people.

## Fact
The average one-way commute in the US is about 27 minutes (US Census, American Community Survey).

## Script

**HOOK, flash-forward (0-3 s).** Two security guards drag Kevin across the office floor by his arms while he yells over his shoulder.
KEVIN: "AND YOUR HAIRLINE IS IN WITNESS PROTECTION!"
Record scratch. Freeze frame.
KEVIN (to camera, still frozen mid-drag): "Oh, hey. Let me back up."

**HOOK INTENSIFIER (3-6 s).** Rewind whoosh to a dingy apartment. Pizza boxes, a sink tower of dishes, a mattress on the floor.
KEVIN (to camera, ring-light voice): "Get ready with me for the job that's slowly killing me."

**GRWM (6-15 s).** Quick cuts with a cheerful influencer beat.
KEVIN: "Skincare." (splashes his face with water from a mug that says nothing, just a cracked mug)
KEVIN: "Breakfast." (sniffs a three-day-old pizza slice, eats it anyway)
KEVIN: "Outfit." (sniffs yesterday's shirt) "...Still in rotation."
NARRATOR: "Kevin makes forty-two thousand a year. His roach makes zero, and pays the same rent."

**COMMUTE + DAYDREAM (15-25 s).** Kevin in gridlock. Dead-eyed.
NARRATOR: "The average American commute is twenty-seven minutes. Kevin spends every one of them like this."
A happy, sunny, ukulele song kicks in. The world turns bright: coastal road, blue sky, Kevin smiling serenely.
He slowly turns the wheel hard right toward the cliff edge... the car launches off... sprouts wings...
glides over the ocean to a beach with a sign showing a palm tree. Kevin in sunglasses, sipping a coconut.
HONK. The song stops mid-note. Back in gray traffic behind a bus.
KEVIN (to camera): "Every. Single. Morning."

**RE-HOOK #1 (25-28 s).** Kevin slumps into his cubicle, opens his laptop.
NARRATOR: "He was gonna make it through the day. Then Chad walked in."

**CHAD (28-36 s).** Chad: huge smile, fist bumps, talking way too loud. Every word comes with a ding-ding sound sting and a camera shake.
CHAD: "GOOOOD MORNING KEVIN! IT'S A BEAUTIFUL DAY TO CIRCLE BACK!"
CHAD: "DID YOU SEE MY STORY? I DID A COLD PLUNGE! IN THE BREAK ROOM SINK!"
Kevin's eye twitches with each word. Zoom closer on every twitch.
NARRATOR (to camera, whispering): "Go on, Kevin. Say it."

**THE SNAP (36-39 s).** Music cuts out. Kevin stands up slowly.
KEVIN: "Chad. You're not a morning person. You're a car alarm with a lanyard."

**RE-HOOK #2, the roast walk (39-50 s).** Kevin walks out through the office, roasting everyone he passes. Hype beat, a whip pan to each person.
To CHAD: "You talk like every sentence got a sponsorship."
To LINDA (coming out of the supply closet): "Linda, you've done more inventory than Costco."
To the IT GUY: "Turn yourself off and on again. Maybe you'll come back with a personality."
To the INTERN: "You work for free and you're STILL overpaid."
To DAVE (in the vent): "And Dave. Pay rent!"
Two security guards grab his arms. This is the hook shot from the start.
KEVIN (to the guard): "And your Bluetooth earpiece isn't connected to ANYTHING!"
KEVIN (to the bald manager): "AND YOUR HAIRLINE IS IN WITNESS PROTECTION!"

**ENDING (50-55 s).** Kevin gets tossed out the front door onto the sidewalk. Silence.
Then the happy ukulele song from the daydream starts playing for real. The sun comes out.
Kevin, lying on the sidewalk, smiles at the camera.
KEVIN: "Anyway, that was my get ready with me. Like and subscribe. I have time now."
NARRATOR: "Kevin was fired at 9:04. He has never been happier."
