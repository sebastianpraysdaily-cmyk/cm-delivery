Kevin puts down his cake, grabs his jacket, and walks very calmly toward the exit. The door is locked. He tries the stairs. They lead straight back to the break room. He tries the elevator. It opens onto the break room. The lights flicker. The printer starts printing by itself. Behind him, the whole team is standing perfectly still, holding cake. Luckily, Kevin still had his badge. Unluckily, his badge now says eleven years.
