Kevin found out his coworker had been at this company for eleven years, and he decided to leave on the spot. It's Monday morning, and the whole team is crowded around a sheet cake in the break room. The boss raises her mug and announces that Dave has been here for eleven years. Everyone claps. Dave just smiles. He hasn't blinked once. The average American stays at a job for about four years. Dave has done almost three of those, back to back.
