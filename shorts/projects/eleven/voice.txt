Kevin found out his coworker had been at this company for eleven years, and he decided to leave on the spot.
It's Monday morning, and the whole team is crowded around a sheet cake in the break room.
The boss raises her mug and announces that Dave has been here for eleven years.
Everyone claps. Dave just smiles. He hasn't blinked once.
The average American stays at a job for about four years. Dave has done almost three of those, back to back.
Kevin puts down his cake, grabs his jacket, and walks very calmly toward the exit.
The door is locked.
He tries the stairs. They lead straight back to the break room.
He tries the elevator. It opens onto the break room.
The lights flicker. The printer starts printing by itself.
Behind him, the whole team is standing perfectly still, holding cake.
Luckily, Kevin still had his badge.
Unluckily, his badge now says eleven years.
You see, nobody plans to stay eleven years. They just stay one more week, about five hundred and seventy times.
Ten years later, a new hire hears that Kevin has been here for eleven years.
Kevin just smiles. He hasn't blinked once.
