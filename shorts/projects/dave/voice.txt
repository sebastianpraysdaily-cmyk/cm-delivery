Kevin found out his coworker had been at this company for eleven years, and decided to escape before lunch. The boss raised her mug and announced it. Dave got a cake shaped like his own chair. The average American stays at a job for about four years. Dave was almost on his third. Kevin quietly grabbed his jacket and speed-walked to the exit. Locked. He tried the window. It was a poster of a window. He tried the elevator. It had one button, and it said Monday. He tried the air vent. Dave was already in there, eating cake. He tried calling in sick. His boss picked up at the next desk and said, feel better. Then the lights went out. Every screen in the office turned on at once, and every screen was Dave. The whole team slowly turned around, holding paper plates, whispering, one more week. Luckily, Kevin found the fire exit. Unluckily, it opened straight into his performance review. You see, nobody plans to stay eleven years. You just say one more week, about five hundred and seventy times. Ten years later, Kevin is in the air vent, eating cake.
