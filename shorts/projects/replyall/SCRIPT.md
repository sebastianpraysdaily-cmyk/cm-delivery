# "Reply All" (DRAFT v1, awaiting approval)

Genre: heist-movie spoof (Ocean's-style crew, laser corridor, countdown), same office cast.
Target: about 52 s, vertical. The narrator only sets up and turns scenes; the characters speak their own lines.

## Fact the joke stands on
In November 2016 a test email went to 840,000 NHS email accounts. Reply-all requests to "take me off this list"
caused about half a billion emails between 08:29 and 09:45. The normal load is 3-5 million a day.
(NHS Digital report via Digital Health News; also The Register, 31 Jan 2017.)

## Cast / voices
NARRATOR (deadpan, steady) · KEVIN (panicky) · LINDA (sweet, terrifying) · DAVE (lives in the vent; from "One More Week")
INTERN (too eager) · IT GUY (monotone)

## Script

**HOOK (0-3 s).** Extreme close-up: a finger slams Enter. The screen whooshes "sent".
KEVIN (tiny voice): "...Did I just hit reply all?"
NARRATOR: "Kevin just emailed 'Linda is a ho'... to the entire company."

**HOOK INTENSIFIER (3-7 s).** Snap zoom across the office to Linda at the coffee machine.
NARRATOR: "Including Linda."
LINDA (sipping): "Just gonna finish my coffee, then check my emails!"

**STAKES / FACT (7-13 s).** A map of glowing inboxes explodes.
NARRATOR: "In 2016, one reply-all hit 840,000 NHS inboxes and caused half a billion emails in about an hour.
Kevin only reached four hundred people. Not enough to crash the server. Just enough to end his life."

**THE PLAN (13-18 s)**
KEVIN: "Okay. We delete it off her computer."
DAVE (sliding open a vent grate): "That's a crime."
KEVIN: "So is her 'inventory.'"

**RE-HOOK #1 (18-20 s).** Linda's coffee cup is half empty.
NARRATOR: "One cup of coffee. And the worst heist crew in America."

**CREW MONTAGE (20-28 s).** Whip pan to each person, freeze pose, music stab.
INTERN: "I'm unpaid. I'll do literally anything."
IT GUY: "Her password is 'password.' Capital P. She's not stupid."
DAVE: "I know the vents. I *live* in the vents."

**THE HEIST (28-36 s).** "Laser grid" spoof: it's just the photocopier's scan light. The intern limbos under it. Kevin crawls the vent.
IT GUY (to Linda, panicked): "Linda! The printer... it's printing!"
LINDA (gasps, turns away): "Oh my god. Finally."
NARRATOR: "The plan was working."

**RE-HOOK #2 (36-39 s).** Hard stop in the music. Linda's cup is empty. She turns back.
NARRATOR: "The plan was not working."
Kevin drops out of the ceiling onto her chair, clicks delete, exhales.
KEVIN: "Deleted. We're clean."

**TWIST (39-47 s).** Linda is standing right behind him, holding a sheet of paper.
LINDA: "Looking for this? I print every email, Kevin. I'm fifty-four."
Kevin swallows. Linda smiles.
LINDA: "Relax. I replied all too."
NARRATOR: "Her reply said one word: 'Correct.'"
The whole office slow-claps. Dave claps from inside the vent.

**LOOP ENDING (47-52 s).** Kevin, shaking, types to Dave: "bro she's actually kind of cool." Enter.
KEVIN (tiny voice): "...Reply all."
Smash cut to the exact opening frame, so the video loops seamlessly.

## Notes
- No on-screen captions or labels. Screen props show only icons ("sent" whoosh, inbox counter as a red dot).
- Every line runs 1-2 s to keep the pace. Callbacks to past episodes: Linda's "inventory", Dave in the vent.
