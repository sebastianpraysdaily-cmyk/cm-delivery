How to use your challenges

  1. Pick one. New to this? Start with a 7-Day Sprint. A quick win beats a big goal you abandon.
  2. Save, then color. Moved money to savings, stuffed an envelope or skipped a purchase? Color a space with that amount. Any order.
  3. Write what it's for. Every page has a "For:" line. Money with a name on it is harder to spend.
  4. Finish, then pick the next. Missed a week? Keep going. Nothing resets.

Sizes: print the Letter file at home, or the A6 file for cash-stuffing binders (4 pages per sheet). Works in GoodNotes and Notability too.

Bonus: your purchase includes the Savings Sprints game app (combos, loot, levels, confetti) and a spreadsheet version. Links are in your download email.
