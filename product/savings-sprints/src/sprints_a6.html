How it works

Long savings challenges ask you to stay excited for a whole year. Most people quit by March. A sprint is short, visual and finished before you get bored. That's the whole idea.

1Pick one sprint. Start with the $50 Weekend Warm-Up if you've never finished a challenge. Winning the first one matters more than the amount.
2Save any bubble's amount. Move it to savings, put cash in an envelope, or skip a purchase that costs about that much. Order doesn't matter.
3Color the bubble. Right away. Seeing it fill up is the reward your brain is looking for.
4Hit a reward step. Every tracker has 25 / 50 / 75 / 100% rewards. Pick them before you start, and keep them cheap or free.
5Finish, then pick the next one. Missed a few days? Keep going. A sprint doesn't reset when life happens.

Every tracker's bubbles add up exactly to its goal. Print at home (Letter or the A6 cash-binder file), use it in GoodNotes or Notability, or use the spreadsheet where bubbles turn color when you tick them.
