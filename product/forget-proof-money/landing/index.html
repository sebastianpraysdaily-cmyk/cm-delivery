Questions

Do I need to switch banks?

Usually not. Many banks let you open extra accounts or "pots" for free in the app. If yours doesn't, a second free bank account works. The guide shows both routes.

My income is irregular. Does this still work?

Yes. You build the split around your lowest month from the last six months, and anything above it goes to your Buffer first. There's a section on this.

Do I need Google Sheets or Excel?

No. The PDF works on paper or in any PDF app. The spreadsheet is a bonus that does the maths for you, and it opens in Google Sheets, Excel or Numbers.
