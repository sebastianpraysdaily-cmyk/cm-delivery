HOW TO GIVE IT
1. Send the film first, with no explanation. Let them watch it twice.
2. Then give the print, framed if you can.
3. Or play the film on the TV at a family dinner. It is 15 seconds; nobody looks away.

PRINTING TIPS
- Choose "actual size" or "fit to page" for 8 x 10 in (20 x 25 cm).
- Matte or lustre photo paper looks best. Avoid glossy if it will hang near a window.

we fix it or refund you in full. You don't need to explain why.
