## 3. FAQ (sales page + listings)

**Do you read my messages?** Our system turns your text into the design automatically. A person only looks at an order if you ask us to fix something.
**Can I use emoji?** Yes, in your messages. Keep your final lines to words; they read best.
**How long does it take?** Usually under 30 minutes for a Print or Film, under an hour for a Collection.
**Who can I make one for?** Mum, Dad and Grandma at launch. Partner, sister and friend versions are coming.
**Is anything shipped?** No. You get files you can print, frame, send or post.
**What if I make a typo?** Reply within 14 days and we'll redo it or refund you in full.
**Can I post the film on social media?** Yes, it's yours to share.
**Is it AI?** The scenes are hand-drawn illustrations, the sound is composed for the film, and every word is yours. Automation just puts it together quickly.

## 7. Engagement workflow (from the blueprint)
- GIF/emoji comment → ask for their version ("What's the text YOUR mum always sends? 👀").
- Story comment → open it to the room ("Who else?") and heart it.
- "Where can I get one?" → reply with the link and pin it.
- Never: fake accounts, bought comments, debates about real people, politics or religion.
