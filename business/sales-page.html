No. The system builds your files automatically. Someone only looks if you ask us to fix something.

Usually under 30 minutes for a Print or Film, under an hour for a Collection.

What if I make a typo?

Reply within 14 days and we'll redo it, or refund you in full.

Is anything shipped?

No. You get files to print, frame, send or post.

Is it AI?

The scenes are hand-drawn, the music is composed for your film, and every word is yours. Automation puts it together quickly.
