## Legal (🟡 news reports 2022–2026)
Take-Two/Rockstar actively enforces: DMCA and trademark notices (incl. via brand-protection firm Ebrand), takedowns of fan projects, a cease-and-desist to a fan account (Apr 2026), even a trademark complaint against a sandwich shop named "Vice City Subs". Selling products with the GTA name, logo, art or screenshots = high takedown/account risk.

## Verdict
Attention: **much better** (small accounts get ~70× ADHD money's median views).
Buying intent: **worse** (save rates 0.5–1% vs 2.46%).
Risk: **high** (trademark), product value: **low** (free wikis), window: **short** (launch spike, we have no accounts yet).

## Follow-up: selling to people who want to make money from GTA 6 (2026-11-11)

### Buyer group 1: content creators (TikTok/YouTube/Twitch)
- Demand for help: "gta 6 content creator" TikToks, median **3,751 views, 0.50% saves** (✅ 10 videos). Weak.
- Free competition: GTATools.net offers 21+ **free** GTA 6 creator tools (thumbnail makers, hook/caption writers, Twitch panels) (🟡).
- Legal: Rockstar's video policy permits fan gameplay uploads framed as **non-commercial** (no money from the footage) (🟡 secondary coverage of policy dated 10 Jan 2025; official page not reachable). Selling "make money from GTA 6 content" products means teaching something Rockstar's policy doesn't clearly permit. Spoiler/cutscene videos are also being taken down.
- Generic gaming-creator assets: "stream overlay" ~72,631 Etsy listings (⚪ RankHero); "gaming tiktok tips" median 39,900 views, 1.70% saves (✅ 5 videos). Real but crowded and not GTA-specific. GTA-themed versions hit trademark risk.

### Buyer group 2: roleplay server owners and modders (FiveM, GTA V)
- Real, legal money: selling FiveM scripts is allowed (Tebex, 15% fee + ~7–8% payment fees; scripts $5–50) (🟡). Tebex creator-code GMV **$61.3M** 2024–2026 (🟡 Tebex's own figure).
- Rockstar's own **Cfx Marketplace** (since Jan 2026): **invite-only**, 39 creators (Sept 2026), GTA V/RDR2 only, revenue share undisclosed (🟡).
- GTA RP TikToks: median 32,950 views (✅ 10 videos). Sellers already pre-announce GTA VI ports for when the PC version arrives (est. early 2027, ⚪).
- Barrier: needs Lua/JS scripting **and** testing on a GTA V PC server. Strong incumbents. We can't test in this container; Sebastian has no dev background.

### Buyer group 3: players wanting in-game money
- "gta 6 money glitch" median 1,370 views (✅). GTA 6 is single-player at launch; GTA Online not before 2027 (🟡). Free wikis/IGN cover it. Weak.

### Verdict
No good product for us right now. Creator kits: weak demand + free tools + policy grey zone. FiveM: real market, but needs skills and testing we don't have, and the GTA 6 version is ~2027. In-game money: no market. Revisit FiveM for GTA 6 if/when a PC release and RP framework appear.
