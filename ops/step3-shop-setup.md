## Recommendation

1. **Etsy first.** All our demand data comes from Etsy (top bundle: 7,002 favorites, 871 reviews), and Etsy brings its own buyers. Cost to start: $0.20 per listing. Some regions also charge a one-time shop opening fee (unverified, Etsy shows it at signup). Opt out of Offsite Ads while sales are under $10k/yr.
2. **Payhip for the TikTok bio link.** Free plan, no monthly fee, keeps the most per sale.
3. Skip Gumroad and Stan for now.
