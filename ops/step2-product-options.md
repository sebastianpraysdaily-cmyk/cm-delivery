Read: **visual "watch it fill up" formats get the reach** (100 envelopes 2.8M, coloring trackers 200k+). **Spreadsheet demos get the saves** (buying intent). **The ADHD angle multiplies both** (26× views vs generic money, from pass 2).

## The 5 options, ranked
1. **ADHD savings challenges you color in** (short 7/14/30-day challenges instead of 52-week ones; printable + digital + a spreadsheet whose cells fill with color). Reach of coloring trackers + intent of ADHD money. Every video = filling in a tracker, so content never runs out. $9–14. Competition: savings challenges are crowded, ADHD-specific short ones rare (🟡).
2. **ADHD money dashboard (Google Sheets)**: one screen, big Safe-to-Spend number, progress bars that fill, colors that change. Highest-intent format (9.5% saves on the top video). Easy to demo by screen recording. $19–29. Competition: 81,304 Etsy "budget spreadsheet google sheets" listings (✅ Etsy count), few ADHD-specific dashboards (🟡).
3. **30-Day No-Spend Reset, ADHD edition**: color-in calendar + daily "do this instead of buying" dopamine swaps + impulse parking lot. Highest median views of any format (52.8k). Fits "no-spend January" timing (⚪ seasonal claim not verified). $9–12.
4. **Forget-Proof Money (current)**: strong save signal for the *message* (2.46%), weakest on-camera format (text pages). Better as the guide inside a bundle than as the hero product.
5. **Dopamine Menu for impulse spenders** (cards + phone wallpapers): high save rate (2.02%) but small reach (1.3k median). Best as a $5 add-on at checkout, not a main product.

## Optimised for sale: price ladder
- $5 add-on at checkout: Dopamine Menu (#5)
- $12 core: savings challenges (#1) *or* no-spend reset (#3)
- $24 bundle: dashboard (#2) + challenges (#1) + Forget-Proof guide (#4) as a bonus
Reason: a cheap entry product converts cold TikTok traffic; the bundle raises order value. Pattern widely used by digital sellers (🟡, not measured by us).
